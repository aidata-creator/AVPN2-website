About This Portal

Welcome to the official AICANWORK website. This portal is designed to provide quick access to essential tools, updates, and classroom material.

AI Can Work! is a program designed to equip service workers with essential AI skills that they need to stay competitive and productive in today’s rapidly changing world of work.

This Artificial Intelligence Upskilling Program for Filipino Workers is part of the AI Opportunity Fund: Asia-Pacific, an initiative by the Asian Venture Philanthropy Network (AVPN) with support from Google.org and the Asian Development Bank. Hapinoy’s Trainors are certified by AI Singapore.
